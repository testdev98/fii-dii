## 🟢 GROUP A: PRICE RISING (↑)

### 1. SUPER BULLISH 🚀🚀
**Combination:** Price ↑ + OI ↑ + FII Buying + DII Buying

**What's Happening:**
- Both FII and DII creating fresh long positions together
- Maximum institutional alignment
- Strong conviction from all big players

**Why It Matters:**
- Strongest possible bullish signal
- All smart money moving in same direction
- Trend has maximum sustainability

**Next Day Expectation:**
- Strong gap-up likely
- Buy on dips strategy works
- Supports will hold strongly
- Momentum continues

**Action:** BUY

### 2. STRONG BULLISH (FII Led) 🚀
**Combination:** Price ↑ + OI ↑ + FII Buying + DII Selling

**Why It Matters:**

- DII selling is just profit booking

- Supports tend to hold
- Follow FII lead

### 3. DIVERGENCE / CAUTION ⚠️

- Watch for reversal
- Don't chase highs

2. **OI** tells strength (new positions vs closing)

4. **DII** tells stability (trend stabilizer)
