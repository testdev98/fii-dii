## 📈 OI Analysis Tab

### What You'll Find

#### 1. **Current Market Phase**
Automatically detects one of 4 phases:

**🚀 Long Buildup** (Price ↑ + OI ↑)
- Fresh long positions being created
- Bulls are aggressive
- **Action: BUY** - Go long, trend will continue

**⚠️ Short Covering** (Price ↑ + OI ↓)
- Short positions being closed
- Rally due to covering, not fresh buying
- **Action: WAIT** - Don't chase, rally is weak

**📉 Short Buildup** (Price ↓ + OI ↑)
- Fresh short positions being created
- Bears are aggressive
- **Action: SELL** - Go short or exit longs

**📊 Long Unwinding** (Price ↓ + OI ↓)
- Long positions being closed
- Profit booking or panic
- **Action: WATCH** - Wait for stabilization

#### 2. **What is Open Interest?**
- Complete definition and explanation
- Why OI matters for trading
- How to interpret OI changes

#### 3. **4 Market Phases Detailed**
Each phase explained with:
- Condition (Price + OI combination)
- Meaning (What's happening)
- Interpretation (Why it matters)
- Action (What to do)
- Next Move (What to expect)

#### 4. **Price vs OI Chart**
- Last 10 days historical data
- Visual correlation between Price and OI
- Key metrics:
  - Average OI
  - Peak OI
  - Low OI
  - Volatility

#### 5. **Call vs Put OI Distribution**
- Visual bar chart of strike-wise OI
- **Call OI (Resistance)**:
  - Max Call OI strike level
  - Total Call OI
  - Acts as resistance

- **Put OI (Support)**:
  - Max Put OI strike level
  - Total Put OI
  - Acts as support

#### 6. **Put-Call Ratio (PCR) Analysis**
- **PCR Value** (Put OI / Call OI)
- **Market Sentiment**:
  - PCR > 1.2 → Bullish (Oversold)
  - PCR 0.8-1.2 → Neutral
  - PCR < 0.8 → Bearish (Overbought)

- **PCR Guidelines**:
  - High PCR = More puts = Expect bounce
  - Low PCR = More calls = Expect correction
  - Extreme PCR = Strong reversal signal

#### 7. **OI-Based Trading Strategies**

**When to Buy (Long):**
1. Long Buildup (Price ↑ + OI ↑)
2. High PCR (>1.2) - Market oversold
3. At Max Put OI - Strong support
4. OI declining in downtrend - Unwinding ending

**When to Sell (Short):**
1. Short Buildup (Price ↓ + OI ↑)
2. Low PCR (<0.8) - Market overbought
3. At Max Call OI - Strong resistance
4. OI declining in uptrend - Covering ending

#### 8. **Quick OI Reference Table**
Easy lookup table showing:
- Price direction
- OI direction
- Market phase
- Meaning
- Recommended action

- Dashboard: SUPER BULLISH

- Dashboard: DIVERGENCE or RETAIL TRAP

- OI: Short Covering (weak rally)

## 📊 Understanding the Data

### FII/DII Numbers

**What the numbers mean:**
- **Positive (+)** = Net Buying (Bullish)
- **Negative (-)** = Net Selling (Bearish)
- **Large numbers (>1000 Cr)** = Strong conviction
- **Small numbers (<500 Cr)** = Weak conviction

**FII Dominance:**
- **>60%** = FII in strong control
- **40-60%** = Balanced
- **<40%** = DII in control

### OI Numbers

**OI Change:**
- **>10%** = Strong buildup/unwinding
- **5-10%** = Moderate activity
- **<5%** = Low activity

**PCR Ratio:**
- **>1.5** = Extremely oversold (Strong buy)
- **1.2-1.5** = Oversold (Buy)
- **0.8-1.2** = Neutral
- **0.6-0.8** = Overbought (Sell)
- **<0.6** = Extremely overbought (Strong sell)
